# Scholaro : The Idea
Bridging the gap between potential and opportunity. In the UK, roughly 21% of students speak English as a second language. Scholaro UK works to break language barriers that hold these vibrant students back from unleashing their full academic potential.You can access FREE resources in the "Resources" section, to explore our simple GCSE revision guides. If you would like to support us, show us some love on youtube, and spread the word!
